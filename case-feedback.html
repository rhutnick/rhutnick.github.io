CAMPMINDER · PROCESS / PRODUCT OPERATIONS

Creating Clarity Around Product Decisions

Customer feedback was fragmented, Product and Engineering decisions could be driven by the loudest voice, Sales could sell against work that was not planned, and leadership struggled to understand product flow.

100%feedback captured100%org engagementMonths → daysreview cycle
THE PROBLEM

What needed to change?

Years of feedback were scattered across teams with no shared definition of value or visible path from idea to product work.

WHAT I DID

Created a centralized idea/feedback portal.

Developed a framework for evaluating value and priority.

Mapped Product and Engineering processes step by step and visualized them.

Created an SOP connecting the idea portal to Engineering work and strategic initiatives.

Worked across CS, Sales, Engineering and leadership to drive adoption.

WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES
Framework design · Process mapping · Decision-making · Change management
RESULT

From opinions to an operating model

Customer feedback was captured in one location, participating organizations engaged with the process, and review time dropped from months to days.
