Helping an Associate PM Find Her Voice

An Associate PM was struggling to get leadership aligned around a product opportunity. The idea was understood, but presentations repeatedly led to pushback and confusion.

1storytelling frameworkCEOrecognized the presentationOutcomeconversation moved to execution
THE PROBLEM

What needed to change?

I believed the underlying idea was not the problem. The presentation started too close to the solution and did not make the customer's experience tangible.

WHAT I DID

Coached her to start with the user rather than the product.

Practiced a sequence: current customer situation → pain → future state → solution.

Helped her practice without taking ownership of the presentation.

She delivered the presentation exceptionally well and leadership moved from confusion to a practical conversation about getting it built.

WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES
Coaching · Communication · Influence · Developing others
RESULT

Help someone else succeed

The CEO stopped the meeting to acknowledge what she had done. She gained credibility with leadership and moved the conversation toward execution.
