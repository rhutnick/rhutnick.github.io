THE PROBLEM

What needed to change?

SMS and KBA were not enough for increasingly high-value transactions, while identity requirements varied across countries and customer types.

WHAT I DID

Conducted discovery with financial-services customers and corporate entities interested in their own verification for employees.

Built the business case and translated discovery into Engineering requirements.

Worked globally across Product, Engineering, customers, vendors and Legal to narrow and deliver scope.

Designed the framework to be reusable by identity providers rather than tied to one country or document.

Ran live prototype demos so customers could experience the verification flow.

WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES
Global scope · Customer discovery · Platform thinking · Business case · Engineering · GTM
RESULT

A reusable foundation

Created the DocuSign Identity Verification Framework used by the Identify offering today, opening additional global digital-agreement use cases.
