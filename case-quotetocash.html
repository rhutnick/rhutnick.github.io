THE MODERN BAY · REVENUE OPERATIONS

Connecting Production to Revenue

The shop completed work but often did not know exactly what to bill. Technicians reconstructed labor and parts from memory weeks or months later.

Weeks → minutesquote-to-cashAt pickuppayment clarityWin-win-wincustomer · tech · owner
THE PROBLEM

What needed to change?

Production and payment were disconnected. Work happened, but the business did not reliably capture that work as billable information while it happened.

WHAT I DID

Implemented a shop-management workflow using ShopMonkey.

Captured labor, parts and taxes as part of the job record.

Connected production to invoicing so the amount owed was clear at pickup.

Reduced the reconstruction burden on technicians and owner and improved customer clarity.

WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES
Systems implementation · Revenue operations · Process redesign
RESULT

Capture the value while doing the work

Quote-to-cash moved from weeks to minutes. Technicians no longer relied on memory to reconstruct jobs, and the business could collect the correct amount at pickup.
