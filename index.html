ABOUT

A lifelong computer person who became obsessed with delivering value to people.

I was taking PCs apart and replacing parts from local computer shops when I was young. At 13, I started working customer-facing jobs—first at a golf course, where I did just about everything from operations to catering and events.

I later worked in a flooring warehouse and learned about logistics, inventory, assets and moving millions of dollars of product. Then I moved into customer service and technical support, where I developed the habits that still shape how I work: empathy, troubleshooting, curiosity and balancing priorities across customers and the organization.

That became the foundation for Product. I realized I loved being in the middle of a problem—understanding what customers needed, figuring out what was technically possible, coordinating the people who could make it happen and delivering something that created value.

After 20+ years around technology and SaaS, I've learned that my sweet spot is where customers, technology, people, process and business outcomes meet.

Whether I'm building a global product framework, creating a partner program, launching a mobile app, fixing a messy shop or rebuilding a customer experience, the pattern is the same: understand the problem, connect the people, create clarity, build a practical path forward and make it happen.
