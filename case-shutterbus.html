THE PROBLEM

What needed to change?

The operation lacked a consistent rhythm around responding, communicating, collecting payment and supporting employees.

WHAT I DID

Set a same-business-day inquiry response goal.

Created a consistent customer communication cadence and advance-payment practices.

Created clearer expectations for seasonal employees.

Generated additional leads proactively rather than relying only on inbound demand.

Closed the payment loop so revenue was collected without chasing customers.
