THE MODERN BAY · OPERATIONS

Turning a Chaotic Shop Into a Production System

Technicians were working on too many vehicles and side projects at once. Priorities changed frequently, parts were late, and context switching consumed time and money.

~90%WIP reduction2 baysfocused model146%*reported efficiency
THE PROBLEM

What needed to change?

On some days three vehicles were being worked on when only one should have been actively progressing. The owner frequently changed priorities.

WHAT I DID

Redesigned the shop around one primary build and an upcoming build.

Introduced twice-weekly blocker and status checks.

Got ahead of parts ordering and vendor work.

Created clear ownership for status, next step and blockers.

Reorganized the physical shop around centralized tools and frequently used materials.

WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES
Operational design · Prioritization · Workflow · Capacity · Frontline change
RESULT

Less WIP. Less noise. More movement.

WIP fell by roughly 90%, production sequencing became clearer, ownership and blockers became visible, and parts/vendor readiness improved. The 146% figure should be explained with its exact calculation before public use.
