DOCUSIGN · FAILURE / PRODUCT LESSON

When a Technical Solution Became a Product Problem

I built a Chrome extension for a strategic partner to increase DocuSign visibility and adoption. The first implementation required users to change Chrome settings normal users would not reasonably expect to change.

View the product →

THE PROBLEM

What needed to change?

The strategic idea was sound, but the implementation created too much friction. Early users told us directly that it was difficult to use.

WHAT I DID

Listened to customer feedback rather than defending the implementation.

Reframed the product around the customer's desired value rather than the technical mechanism.

Worked through the experience changes needed to remove unnecessary friction.

Carried the lesson forward: technology should enable value, not become the customer's problem.

WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES
Customer empathy · Course correction · Product judgment · Usability
RESULT

A failure that became a success

The product was turned around into a successful adoption channel. The public Chrome Web Store listing currently shows roughly 300,000 users and 1,200 ratings.
